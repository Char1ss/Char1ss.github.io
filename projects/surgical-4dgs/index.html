4D Reconstruction of Suturing

Reconstructing a changing workspace from four camera views.

  • 4DGS
  • Four-camera capture
  • Self-collected data

My contribution

Work on the multi-camera capture and 4D reconstruction pipeline within a collaborative project.

Project focus

Capture setup and reconstructed suturing sequence

Research question

How can a compact multi-camera setup capture and reconstruct a fine-grained procedure with moving hands, instruments and frequent occlusion?

Approach

Our team records the suturing procedure from four camera views and reconstructs the temporal scene using 4D Gaussian Splatting. The work connects data acquisition with dynamic reconstruction and inspection of the resulting sequence.

Current work

A reconstruction prototype on self-collected suturing recordings. The project extends earlier practical work with dynamic Gaussian reconstruction on public datasets.

Ongoing research on dynamic reconstruction from self-collected, four-camera recordings.
