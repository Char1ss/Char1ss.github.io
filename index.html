Zhixiang Fan

Spatial intelligence · Design · Interaction

I am a PhD student in Design at Tsinghua University’s Academy of Arts & Design, with a background in landscape architecture. I study how people understand, create and interact with three-dimensional environments.

I focus on translating spatial design knowledge into forms that are computable and interactive, enabling reconstructed or generated 3D environments to be understood, inspected, and modified.

A medical reconstruction project using four camera views and self-collected recordings of a doctor performing suturing. We use 4D Gaussian Splatting to reconstruct the changing scene over time.

Design knowledge & human–AI creation · 2026

Design Skills for Chinese Gardens

I describe spatial design rules in natural language and organize them into reusable Design Skills that guide a lightweight LLM to construct scenes and models. Examples include Chinese gardens, temples and imagined mountain landscapes.
